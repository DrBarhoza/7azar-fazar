# 7azar Fazar — Family Quiz Battle 🎯

A party quiz game for two teams, in the spirit of classic Arabic TV quiz shows.
One person hosts on a shared screen (laptop or TV); teams answer out loud and
the host judges.

Open the site, name the two teams, pick up to six topics, and play. It installs
as an app — on iPhone and iPad use Safari's Share ▸ *Add to Home Screen*, on
Android use Chrome's *Install app* — and works offline once loaded.

**How a question goes.** Both teams answer out loud, then the host presses
**Reveal answer**. Only then do the judging buttons appear: *Correct* (full
points), *Wrong* (no points), or *Steal* (half points to the other team). Year
questions accept a year either side.

Around 3,175 questions across eight topics: Geography, History, Hollywood,
Animals, Medicine, Soccer, Countries, and Guess the Country (flags-by-emoji,
map shapes and landmark photos).

You can load your own question bank from a `.docx` or `.json` file — press
**Load a different question bank** on the setup screen.
